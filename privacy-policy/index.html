Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GoBizIT (“GoBizIT”, “we”, “us”, “our”), operating the website www.gobizit.ai, collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website, fill in our forms, book a call, or agree to receive text (SMS) messages from us.

1. Who we are

GoBizIT is an AI-driven CRM and RevOps consultancy based in Paterna (Valencia), Spain. You can contact us at info@gobizit.ai or +1 424 208 0299 (USA) / +34 600 266 148 (Europe).

2. Information we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, email address, mobile phone number, company name and job title.
  • Request details: the message or service interest you enter in our forms, and the date and time of any discovery call you book.
  • SMS consent records: whether you opted in to SMS, the date, time, source (web form) and IP address of your opt-in, and any opt-out (STOP) requests.
  • Communication history: messages you send us and that we send you by email, SMS, WhatsApp or phone.
  • Technical data: browser type, device information, IP address and pages visited, collected through standard website logs and cookies.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and follow up on the information or services you asked about.
  • To schedule, confirm and send reminders for discovery calls and meetings.
  • To provide customer support and service updates to our clients.
  • To send SMS messages only to people who have given express consent (see section 4).
  • To operate, secure and improve our website and services, and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

4. SMS messaging and opt-in data

If you opt in to receive SMS messages from GoBizIT, we use your mobile number to send lead follow-up messages about the services you requested, appointment confirmations and reminders, and customer support messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP, and get help by replying HELP.

Consent to receive SMS is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except with the service providers that deliver the messages on our behalf (such as our SMS carrier/platform provider), solely to provide the messaging service.

5. How we share information

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business, under contract and only for the purposes above, for example our CRM and forms provider (Zoho), our communications/SMS provider (Twilio), our website host, and our professional advisers, or when required by law.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. SMS consent and opt-out records are kept to demonstrate compliance with messaging rules.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls, to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU GDPR), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, object to or restrict its processing, or withdraw consent at any time. Email info@gobizit.ai to make a request. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page with its “Last updated” date.
